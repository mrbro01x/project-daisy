## 📖 About Project Daisy

**Project Daisy** is an open-source repository dedicated to preserving and sharing pristine, professionally formatted EPUB editions of classic Bengali literature. 

While public-domain digital Bengali libraries are widely available, many suffer from formatting inconsistencies, OCR artifacts, broken internal navigation, or poor typographic standards. Project Daisy solves this by delivering clean, standards-compliant, and beautifully typeset EPUB files designed for an optimal reading experience across all modern e-readers and applications.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Typographic Precision:** Clean XHTML/CSS styling, accurate punctuation, and support for advanced digital typography standards.
* **Flawless Navigation:** Fully verified NAV and NCX documents ensuring seamless table-of-contents support, chapter jumping, and bookmarking.
* **Cross-Device Compatibility:** Tested for flawless rendering on major reading platforms (Apple Books, Calibre, KOReader, Google Play Books, and dedicated e-ink devices).
* **Open Source & Accessible:** Freely accessible for personal reading, archival, and community-driven curation.

---

## 📥 Access & Usage

You can download any `.epub` file directly from this repository and open it using your preferred e-reader. If you use **Calibre**, these files can be easily synced to your mobile device or e-ink reader without any conversion issues.

## 💡 Reporting Issues & Suggestions

We strive to maintain the highest standard of typographic precision and accuracy in our EPUB archive. If you spot a typo, formatting error, or any discrepancy in a book, please let us know! 

### How to Report an Error:

1. **Open an Issue:** Navigate to the [Issues](../../issues) tab of this repository and click on **New Issue**.
2. **Select the Template:** Choose the **"Book Error / Correction Report"** template.
3. **Provide Details:** Fill out the requested information:
   * **Book Title & Author**
   * **Chapter / Section / Location**
   * **Description of the issue** (What it currently says vs. what it should be)
4. **Submit:** Click **Submit new issue**, and we will review and update the EPUB file accordingly.

Your contributions help keep this archive pristine for readers everywhere!
